Blitz

Delete your account

Immediately, and for good.

From the app

  1. Open Blitz.
  2. Tap Preferences (the gear, top right).
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

This removes your account, your words, your pictures, your grammar history and your streak from our servers, and clears the copy on that device. Nothing is kept.

Without the app

Write to blitz@4oli.com from the email address you signed in with, and ask us to delete the account. We answer within 30 days, usually the same day.

We ask from that address only so nobody else can delete your words.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and profile picture.
  • Every word, with its meanings and pictures.
  • The grammar history and the days you practised.
  • Every signed-in session, on every device.

What is kept

Nothing tied to you. Definitions and speech clips that were made while you used Blitz are shared, anonymous, and belong to the word rather than to you; they stay so the next person does not pay for them again.
