Privacy

Last updated 19 October 2026.

Blitz is a vocabulary app. It keeps the words you choose, what they mean, and any picture you give them. This page says exactly what is stored, where, and how to delete it.

Without an account

Everything you add stays on your device. Words, pictures, the grammar history and the days you practised are stored in a local database inside the app. None of it leaves the device, and nobody else can see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

With an account

Signing in with Google adds three things:

  • Who you are. Google gives us your name, email address and profile picture when you sign in. We keep them to show you which account you are in. We never see your Google password.
  • Your words. The words, meanings, pictures and grammar history you save are copied to our servers so they appear on your other devices. Pictures are stored under a hash of their bytes.
  • When you practised. The dates on which you answered at least one practice question, so the streak follows you between devices. The questions and answers themselves are not kept.

What is sent to a model

Two features ask a language model for help, and both need an account:

  • Writing checks. When you press Check, the text you wrote in that box is sent to OpenAI to be marked. Nothing else about you is sent, and the text is not stored by us. The sentences with a grammar mistake are kept in your grammar history, on your devices and in your account, so you can look at them again.
  • Definitions. When a word you pick has no written definition, our server asks a model for one. Only the word itself is named; the model never receives anything you typed about yourself. The answer is stored for everyone, not tied to your account.

Writing checks are limited per day, and so are definitions. Blitz does not train any model on your text.

Speech

Words read aloud are made on our server and the clip is kept so it is never made twice. Only the word and its language are sent; the recording is not tied to any account and carries no information about who asked for it.

What is never done

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone beyond the processors named here.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts the service, the database and the pictures.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • OpenAI checks written texts when you press Check.
  • Workers AI writes definitions for words that have none.

Deleting everything

In the app: Preferences → Delete account. That removes your account, your words, your pictures, your grammar history and your streak from our servers, and clears the copy on the device, immediately and for good. You can also ask from this page.

Without an account, deleting the app is enough.
