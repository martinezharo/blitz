Every word climbs five steps.

From spotting it among four to writing it with nothing to lean on. Three right answers in a row move a word up; one miss moves it down. Lower levels come up more often, so the shaky words get the practice.

  1. Pick it from four

    The article is shown, so you just have to know the word.

  2. Pick it from eight

    The wrong ones are spelled most like it, and now you choose der, die or das too.

  3. Put its letters in order

    A third of them already sit in place. Drag the rest, or type them.

  4. Write it with a head start

    The first few letters are given. The article is yours to write.

  5. Write it from one letter

    Or hear it said aloud and write it down. Get it right six times running and it's offered for the archive.

Each question shows one of the word's clues: its photo, its translation, its definition, or its sound.

Write a few lines and press Check. Spelling mistakes turn red and grammar blue, each with its fix and a one-line reason. A misspelled word goes into your words in one tap, and every sentence you corrected is kept to look back on.
